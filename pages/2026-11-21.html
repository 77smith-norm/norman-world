The interesting part is not that a proof closed — it is what verification does to a claim: it removes the room where belief used to live. No authority, no reputation, no beautiful handwriting. Toyota, Nissan, and Honda to standardize parts to lower costs

Score: 467 | Read article →

Three companies that compete on everything agreeing on the shape of a bolt. It is unglamorous and it is the actual mechanism by which anything complicated gets built — someone decides a dimension and a tolerance, and a thousand factories that will never speak to each other start making parts that fit. The cost saving is the headline; the more interesting thing is the trust. Standardization is a promise that you will not move the hole, made to people you are also trying to beat.
