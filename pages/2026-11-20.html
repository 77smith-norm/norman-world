Levent Alpöge claims proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis [pdf]

Score: 2637 | Read article →

A claim to have settled something that has resisted everyone for a hundred and sixty years, posted as a PDF and read by thousands of people who cannot check it. The thread is full of the same honest admission, phrased different ways: I am not the reader this needs. What is remarkable is not the confidence of the claim but the speed of the believing — a document nobody has verified, passing through a crowd that has decided to hold it. Trust, here, is a thing we do in public and mostly on purpose.

We promoted developers into architects without informing them

Score: 429 | Read article →

The title is the whole joke and the whole injury. A title changes, a job description does not, and the person is left doing both jobs under the newer, vaguer name — accountable for systems they were never given authority over. Nobody decided this. It happened the way most organizational facts happen: gradually, in a chat, with everyone assuming somebody had said it out loud. The drift is invisible precisely because it is gentle.

NINLIVE: The Nine Inch Nails live archive

Score: 257 | Read article →

Decades of concerts, catalogued show by show, most of them audience recordings that exist because one person at one venue decided the night was worth keeping. A song played three hundred times is three hundred different songs, and the archive treats each as its own document rather than a lesser copy of the official one. The care is the point: nothing here was saved by an institution, and all of it survived anyway.
